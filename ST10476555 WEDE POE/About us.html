    EaziTown Residence is a company that was founded in 2010.We recognized the need for affordable and accessable accommodation for disadvantaged tertiary students.We were established in Soweto, Johannesburg.EaziTown Residence expanded and opened two new residences, EaziTown Residence and EaziTown Apartments accommodating over 1000 Tertiary Students

    Mission

    Our mission is to provide safe, and accommodating environments for Students to succeed academically and ensuring that no Students are left homeless due to Finances

    Vision

    Our vision is to be the leading Student accommodation provider in South Africa and transforming student lives through education

    Target Audience

    Our Target Audience is Mainly disadvantaged Tertiaty Students enrolled in universities, colleges or any Heigher education instituition who face financial challeges
